Our History

From Street Stall to Citywide Favorite

2018

A Small Stall in Saddar

KarachiBites started as a single grill cart, serving rolls and burgers to the after-work crowd.

2020

First Sit-Down Branch

Demand grew fast enough to open our first proper outlet, complete with a full kitchen and dine-in seating.

2022

Online Ordering Launches

We brought the menu online so all of Karachi could order delivery straight to their door.

2024

15+ Branches Citywide

From Clifton to North Nazimabad, KarachiBites became a fixture in neighborhoods across the city.

Today

Still Grilling, Still Local

Same fresh ingredients, same bold flavor — now with a menu that keeps growing.
